# Code of conduct

Agent Debate Engine is a technical project built around disagreement, criticism, and uncertainty.
Participation must keep those pressures directed at ideas and evidence, not people.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and willing to revise a claim when evidence changes.
- Separate observed behavior from assumptions and preferences.
- Give credit for contributed ideas, code, prompts, tests, and documentation.
- Minimize exposure: use synthetic reproductions and remove private or identifying data.
- Respect the project's safety boundaries even when proposing experimental provider behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing another person's private information or private project artifacts.
- Posting credentials, sensitive prompts, exploit details, or malicious payloads in public threads.
- Using project spaces to encourage unauthorized access, provider abuse, or bypass of explicit safety
  controls.
- Sustained disruption after a maintainer asks that a discussion return to evidence and scope.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces
officially representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

For an urgent safety issue, follow `SECURITY.md`. For a conduct report that cannot safely be public,
open a minimal issue asking the maintainers to establish private contact; do not include sensitive
details in that issue. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and hand a report to an
uninvolved maintainer whenever possible.
